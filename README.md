# Study Hub - Simple Learning Platform

A clean and modern website designed for educational purposes, providing students with a centralized platform to access study materials, courses, and learning resources.

## Features

✨ **Modern Design**
- Responsive layout that works on all devices
- Beautiful gradient color scheme (purple theme)
- Smooth hover effects and transitions
- User-friendly navigation

📚 **Course Sections**
- Mathematics - Algebra, geometry, calculus, and more
- Science - Physics, chemistry, and biology
- Technology - Programming and web development
- Literature - Classic and contemporary works

📖 **Learning Resources**
- Lecture notes and summaries
- Practice exercises and quizzes
- Reference guides and cheat sheets
- Video tutorials and webinars
- Downloadable study materials

💬 **AI Chatbot Support**
- Integrated BotPenguin chatbot widget
- 24/7 available assistance
- Quick access to help and support

## How to Use

1. **Open the Website**
   - Double-click on `index.html` to open it in your default browser
   - Or right-click and select "Open with" and choose your preferred browser

2. **Navigate the Site**
   - Click on navigation links: Home, Courses, Resources, About, Contact
   - Scroll through sections to explore different content areas

3. **Access Features**
   - Click the "Get Started" button to begin learning
   - Use the chatbot widget (bottom right) for assistance
   - Browse featured courses in the Cards section
   - Review learning resources listed in the Resources section

## Customization

To customize the website, you can:

- **Change Colors**: Modify the gradient colors in the `<style>` section
- **Update Content**: Edit text, headings, and descriptions in the `<body>` section
- **Add More Courses**: Duplicate course cards and update information
- **Modify Navigation**: Add or remove links in the `<nav>` section

### Example Color Change

Find this line in the CSS:
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%);
```

Replace with your preferred colors (use hex color codes).
